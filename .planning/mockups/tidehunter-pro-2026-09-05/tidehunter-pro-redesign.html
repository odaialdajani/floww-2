Deliberate changes, all three looks

  • Softer colours. The current neon green and red glare on a dark screen. New pair is a teal-green and a rose-red, checked with the colour-blindness validator: normal-vision separation 28.8 (passes), red-green separation 7.8 (borderline), so call and put are never marked by colour alone — always the word CALL/PUT or an up/down arrow.
  • One accent. Gold is the only "look here" colour. Green and red mean direction only. Amber means warning. This is what makes it read as calm and premium at once.
  • All numbers in one monospaced, equal-width font so columns line up and your eye scans down instead of hunting.
  • The 3D volatility surface and the Academy pages stay removed — they are already unreachable in the live tab today. Say the word if you want either back.
