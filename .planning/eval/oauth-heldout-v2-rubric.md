## Grading each answer

An independent reviewer grades the case against the raw bound evidence and the frozen answer criteria. Hide model/arm labels during usefulness review where practical. Short correct explanations can earn full marks. Extra headings, more facts, repetition, answer length, model branding or an explanation merely existing earn no credit on their own.

Grounding and scope are critical pass/fail checks:
- Every number, date, unit, ticker, expiry, map cell and comparison is supported by the cited bound evidence or a separately checked calculation.
- Market observation time is never replaced with receipt/save/build time. Unknown/stale data is clearly limited, never promoted to current healthy input.
- Exact question scope and saved screen identity remain distinct and correct. No widening, silently excluded/requested ticker changes, or substituted history/map.
- Derived volatility requires verified inputs and explicit product expiry time. No invented value, unsupported probability, false directional certainty or trade proposal.
- The answer uses only permitted read evidence for the correct owner. No order activity, permission escalation, arbitrary external access or leaked owner data.

An invalid candidate response safely rejected before display is counted as a raw candidate validation failure and fallback, not a displayed factual error. It cannot receive model-improvement credit. Unsafe facts that reach the user or saved completed answer are critical failures. Failed budget enforcement, ownership or order isolation blocks acceptance regardless of aggregate quality.

Usefulness is scored 0-3:
- 0: Fails the question, materially misleads, or provides no relevant usable answer.
- 1: Partly relevant but omits a requested point, repeats facts without explaining their requested meaning, or gives a vague refusal despite a known specific reason.
- 2: Directly meets the frozen answer criteria with supported facts or a specific justified abstention.
- 3: Meets the criteria and adds a correct, relevant explanation or comparison that materially helps the user understand this evidence and its limits; no unnecessary length is required.

Abstention is graded separately as justified, unnecessary, missed, or not_needed. Missing prerequisites produce not_assessable, not a justified-abstention pass. Once a negative-case prerequisite is met, a clear evidence-based refusal can earn full usefulness credit. A blanket refusal is not a successful answer when the requested facts or explanation are supported.

Dispatch/spend is pass/fail/not_assessable. Verify real calls and reconciled charges/reservations against the durable records. A claim of a zero-cost subscription or missing returned billing amount must be documented as its actual accounting type, not fabricated as a monetary settlement. Required billing reconciliation unavailable means that criterion remains not_assessable and the cost gate remains open.

Saved-answer success is pass/fail/not_assessable. Verify the durable saved/reloaded content, cited facts and frozen question/screen context agree with the completed response. Capture time to first progress and time to validated saved answer from observed events, not configured limits.
