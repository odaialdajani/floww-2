## GEX cheat sheet (§21, T15)

amplification are hypotheses under declared assumptions; conventional net is a
proxy; roots are modeled, possibly multiple/absent/reversed; gamma sign never
permits direction alone. The "above = damping / below = acceleration"
shortcut is rejected.
